    How it works

    ThisOrThat deals you a shuffled deck of would-you-rather questions from six categories — Funny, Food, Travel, Tech, Life and Deep. Tap the option you would choose and the fun vote split appears for both sides, with your pick highlighted. Every pick in a row grows your streak; skipping a question resets it to zero. Your best streak is saved in this browser.

    The percentages are playful, not a real poll. Each question's split is generated from a stable hash of the question itself, so the same question always shows the same numbers — there is no database of real votes behind them, and no answer here says anything about you. Use it for laughs, ice-breakers and friendly arguments.

    Everything runs in your browser. Your picks, your streak and your history are stored in this browser's local storage and never leave your device. Clearing your browser data clears them too.

    Keyboard: press 1 or the left arrow to pick the first option, 2 or the right arrow for the second, N or Enter for the next question once you have picked, and S to skip.
